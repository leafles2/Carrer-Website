Job Market & Earnings

Job Market

The national job market for Web and Digital Interface Designers shows steady and positive growth across the United States. According to employment data, the country supported 128,900 jobs in 2024, and that number is projected to increase to 137,900 by the year 2034. This upward trajectory reflects a 7% percent change over the decade, resulting in approximately 9,100 annual projected job openings for aspiring professionals entering the field.

Earnings

When examining annual compensation, earning potential varies significantly between different wage brackets and geographic regions. High earners in Washington bring in an annual wage of $217,290, which outpaces the national high wage benchmark of $201,550. Similarly, the median annual wage sits at $130,480 in Washington compared to a national median of $104,000, while low-range annual wages are recorded at $60,580 locally versus $53,750 nationwide.
