Education Requirements

What is needed?

When looking at the educational background of professionals in this career, the vast majority hold a Bachelor's degree at 54%. Additional education levels include 16% with a Master's degree, 11% with some college experience but no degree, 9% holding an Associate's degree, and 6% with a high school diploma or equivalent. A smaller percentage of workers have achieved a doctoral or professional degree at 2%, while 1% have less than a high school diploma
