UI/UX Design

Why did I choose this career?

I decided to pursue UI/UX design because my teacher introduced our class to the fundamentals of web design. Around the same time, I worked on a side project helping design the user interface for a software launcher. As I dove deeper into these tasks, I slowly realized how much I enjoyed the creative and problem-solving aspects of design, long before I even knew the field's name. Later, while researching career options through school resources, I discovered that this passion translates into a professional career known as UI/UX design, which offers both strong earning potential and work I genuinely enjoy.
